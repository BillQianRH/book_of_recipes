# Mayonaise

Some history

## Ingredients

- 1 large egg
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar or white wine vinegar
- 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt, or more to taste
- 1 cup (240ml) safflower oil or vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice, optional

## Instructions

- Blend everything
- Enjoy!
