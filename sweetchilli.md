# Sweet Chilli
Sweet chilli sauce is a popular condiment in Thai, Afghan, Malaysian, and Western cuisine.
It is commonly made with red chilli peppers, rice wine vinegar, sometims garlic, sometimes fish sauce, and a sweetening ingredient such as fruit or honey. 
Read more at the [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_chili_sauce).

## Ingredients
* 10 red chillis
* 2 tsp oil
* 5 tbsp rice wine vinegar
* 4 tbsp honey

## Instructions
* chop chillies
* place a saucepan on medium heat
* heat oil and then chillies
* add vineger and then honey
* cook for 3 minutes
* reduce heat and gently cook for 10 minutes
